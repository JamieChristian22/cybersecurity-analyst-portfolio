# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This project welcomes people who want to learn, review, and improve security engineering in a respectful and responsible way. Contributors, reviewers, and visitors should feel welcome regardless of background or experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be courteous and constructive when discussing technical findings.
- Distinguish verified evidence from hypotheses, simulated outcomes, and assumptions.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and differences in experience.
- Use only authorized systems and synthetic or appropriately sanitized test data.
- Give feedback that can be acted upon, with reproducible steps where possible.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, threats, personal attacks, disclosure of private information, and encouragement of unauthorized intrusion or misuse are not accepted.

## Reporting concerns

Raise community conduct concerns privately with the maintainer through an available non-public contact channel. Do not post personal information, credentials, or sensitive disclosures in public issues. The maintainer may remove inappropriate content, close discussions, or restrict participation when needed. No guaranteed response time is promised.

## Scope

These standards apply to project-related issues, pull requests, discussions, and other community interactions.
